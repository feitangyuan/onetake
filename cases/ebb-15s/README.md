## How it was made (from the composition's header)

```text
CONCEPT — one take, and the light carries the camera. ebb is a focus timer (fictional); its one object is a field of warm
light that lives under everything and is time made visible. The camera never cuts: it starts inside the digits, pulls out
to the Mac app, rides the light up while a session runs, punches onto 00:00, and travels under the flood while it drains
in rings — the rings open on the phone, whose lock screen is the break in silk. It pushes onto the Live Activity, whips
down onto the day's sessions (three rows of silk at three depths, drifting against each other), dollies along them, and
pulls back while they fly into the week. The week sinks into the horizon as light, and the name rises out of it.

The camera is keyed (OM.camera) plus shake, parallax (OM.view depth) and a far lattice — no OM.rig, so it runs on lite.
```

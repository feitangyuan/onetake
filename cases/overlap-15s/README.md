## How it was made (from the composition's header)

```text
CONCEPT — a two-colour risograph print, pulled by two people, with a message neither half carries. A pink cursor, "you",
and a blue one, "them", grab a drum each at opposite edges of one sheet and pull toward each other: pink lays "you" and
a circle of random dots, blue lays "them" and a second circle. The circles share one grid and one coin per cell: pink
inks heads; blue inks tails, except inside a hidden ampersand, where it inks heads too. Each plate alone is the same
noise; only exactly in register do the & cells land together and multiply to violet. The blue lands out of register, so
nothing shows. A loupe finds dots that miss each other and a doubled mark; "them" clicks beside the mark, the blue snaps
into register and "you & them" appears. The print run is every other pair it is for; "them" yanks the stack away; the two
pull the name, over + lap, and meet where the r crosses the l; the camera, locked off until then, pushes in on that
crossing and tilts down to the line. Fictional product (overlap, a shared canvas for two). Zero assets; one canvas; every
value a function of t.

CAMERA — locked off (a copy stand over the press bed) until the name is in register, then one operator (OM.rig): into
the crossing of r and l, down to the line, back out to the card. Before that the optics are one rack focus and one loupe. MOTION — mechanical:
drums pulled in (u²) and rolled out (quadOut), feeds that slam, dots that grow in steps; two springs, both registrations.
PALETTE — cream stock on a warm grey bed; fluorescent pink and riso blue, overprinted by multiply.
```

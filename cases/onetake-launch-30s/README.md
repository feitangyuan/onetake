## How it was made (from the composition's header)

```text
CONCEPT — three prompts, three films, one take. The prompt bar is the spine: typed three times, shorter each time
(a sentence → four words → one word), and each prompt visibly becomes a finished mini-film for a new fictional product,
in its own palette and motion vocabulary: bar 1 opens into the Mac window, bar 2 opens into the Hush frame, bar 3
collapses into a line that shoots across the desk and opens into the LED screen. The films sit on one warm paper desk;
the camera never cuts, and at the end it pulls back until all three are playing at once over the bar, whose block
cursor writes the name in one stroke.

v8 (after v7's 「看起来只有一个 prompt」): the camera lands on every bar, and every send is the birth of its film.
Richer films: Dayline gets an event sheet on iPhone, Hush a charging-case product shot. The prompt glyph is a
terminal's: a grey chevron and a solid block cursor. After the loop the three films gather into one row, all the
height of the Hush frame, centred over the bar.
```
